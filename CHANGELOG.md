# Changelog

## 0.1.0 – first release

The first public release, for the FuriPhone FLX1s with FuriOS. Musette is built
on SimpMusic 2.7.0.

- Home: YouTube Music's shelves (quick picks, mixes, new releases, charts),
  loaded page by page
- Search with suggestions, history and filters for songs, videos, albums,
  artists and playlists
- Album, playlist and artist pages
- Library: liked songs, followed artists, most played, downloads, your
  playlists, the playlists and mixes of your YouTube account, saved albums
  and playlists, recently added
- Mini player and a Now Playing sheet with seek, shuffle, repeat, like and
  the queue
- Likes sync to YouTube when you are signed in; signed out, they stay in your
  local favourites
- Playback controls and track information in Phosh and on the lock screen
  (MPRIS)
- Music keeps playing when the window is closed. Start Musette again to show
  the window, or choose **Quit** in the window menu
- Sign-in through Google's page, several accounts, sign-in with a cookie as a
  fallback
- The interface comes in every language SimpMusic is translated into
- Packages: a DEB in the APT repository [deb.xvlad.com](https://deb.xvlad.com)
  and a Flatpak in [flatpak.xvlad.com](https://flatpak.xvlad.com), both for
  ARM64, with a reduced Java runtime inside
